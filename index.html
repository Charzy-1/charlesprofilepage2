A little about me

I'm a self-taught developer who fell into frontend work through a design detour — I kept wanting to build the interfaces I was mocking up, not just draw them. These days I split my time between component work, accessibility fixes, and arguing with myself about flexbox versus grid.

I care most about interfaces that feel considered: layouts that hold up at any width, code that reads clearly six months later, and motion that guides rather than distracts.
